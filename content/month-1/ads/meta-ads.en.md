## Variant A — The parent who writes at night
- **Headline:** Do not leave the parent who writes at night unanswered
- **Text:** A parent who writes "Do you have an IELTS course?" at 11:40 pm may move to another center by morning. QeydAI replies instantly in Azerbaijani and books the trial lesson. The pilot is open to learning centers in Baku.
- **CTA:** Request a demo
- **Targeting:** Baku and Absheron; age 25-55; roles: center owner, director, manager; interests: education, small business.
- **Visual:** A night chat on a phone screen with the "Enrollment confirmed" card.

## Variant B — Never promise a full group
- **Headline:** Never promise a seat in a full group
- **Text:** When two people try for the last seat at the same moment, who wins? In QeydAI the program checks open seats, only one is accepted and the other is offered another time. Duplicate sign-ups are blocked too.
- **CTA:** See how it works
- **Targeting:** Same as Variant A; add interests: enrollment software, CRM.
- **Visual:** "2 seats left" and "Group is full, Wednesday 18:00" as two messages side by side.

## Variant C — Your managers' time
- **Headline:** Let your managers talk only to ready buyers
- **Text:** QeydAI answers questions about price, schedule and "can I come?". It never invents an answer; it passes it to a manager. You get a notification only when someone has enrolled.
- **CTA:** Apply for the pilot
- **Targeting:** Baku; age 28-50; roles: sales manager, enrollment staff, director.
- **Visual:** A notification on a manager's phone: "New enrollment: Saturday 11:00".
