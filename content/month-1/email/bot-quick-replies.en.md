# Bot quick replies (Telegram / WhatsApp) — EN

All figures and times are **examples**. In a real chat, price, time and seat count come from the center's approved table (`Kurslar_ve_Qrafik`, `Sinag_Dersleri`). Information not in the table is never given.

## 1. Greeting
"Hello! Welcome to Sample Learning Center. Which course interests you: IELTS, general English or math?"

## 2. Price question
"The IELTS course is 180 AZN per month, 3 lessons a week. Would you like me to book a trial lesson?"

## 3. Schedule question
"Current trial lessons: Saturday 11:00 (2 seats left), Wednesday 18:00 (5 seats left). Which suits you?"

## 4. Wants to sign up
"Great! Please send your name and phone number to enroll."

## 5. Successful enrollment (only after the record is written)
"Your enrollment is confirmed: IELTS, Saturday 11:00. Reply to this message if you would like a reminder before the lesson."

## 6. Group is full
"Unfortunately this group is full. The nearest open seat is Wednesday at 18:00. Shall I book you there?"

## 7. Duplicate inquiry
"There is already an enrollment on this number: IELTS, Saturday 11:00. If you want to change it, tell me and I will pass it to a manager."

## 8. Phone number is wrong
"I did not fully understand the number. Please write it like this: 050 123 45 67."

## 9. Unknown question / discount
"I am checking the exact details with our manager and will write back by 6:00 pm today."

## 10. Outside working hours
"I received your message. Our manager will write after 10:00 am. While you wait, would you like to see the courses?"

## 11. Complaint / upset customer
"We are sorry for the trouble. I am passing your question to a manager right away, and they will contact you."
