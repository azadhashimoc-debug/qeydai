# Trial lesson sign-ups: how to handle open seats and duplicate registrations

The Saturday 11:00 group has 8 seats. On Thursday three people write "I want to sign up" at the same moment. Whom do you promise a seat? If the answer is not "whoever registered first", this article is for you.

## Three common problems

**Promising a seat in a full group.** A manager says "there is room" from memory, but someone else already registered in the morning. The visitor arrives and hears "no seats".

**The same person registering twice.** A parent writes on WhatsApp and on Instagram, creating two records. The group looks fuller than it is.

**A past or nonexistent time.** "Saturday 11:00" in an old message has already passed, yet the customer still shows up.

## Simple rules

1. **One source.** Track open seats in a single table.
2. **Check first, confirm after.** Send "Your enrollment is confirmed" only after the record was written successfully.
3. **Check duplicates by phone number.** 050 123 45 67, +994501234567 and 0501234567 are the same number.
4. **Offer an alternative when full.** "This group is full; the next trial lesson is Wednesday at 18:00."
5. **Block sign-ups for past dates.**
