# How to answer a price question on WhatsApp: sample messages for learning centers

"How much does it cost?" is the first question a learning center hears most often. Sending only a number and going quiet ends the chat. Adding value and a next step next to the number keeps it going.

> The figures below are examples. Write your own center's approved price.

## Three parts of a good reply

1. **A clear number:** "The IELTS course is 180 AZN per month."
2. **What is included:** "3 lessons a week, up to 10 people per group."
3. **The next step:** "Shall I book you into the Saturday 11:00 trial lesson?"

## Four examples

**Formal and short:**
"Hello! The IELTS course is 180 AZN per month, 3 lessons a week. We have a trial lesson on Saturday at 11:00. Shall I book you in?"

**For a parent:**
"Hello! How old is your child and what is the current level? I will suggest the right group, then give you the exact price."

**When the price feels high:**
"I understand. Please come to a trial lesson first, see it, then decide. The trial lesson is free." (Write this only if it is true at your center.)

**When you do not know the answer:**
"I am checking the exact details with our manager and will write back by 6:00 pm today."

## What not to write

- Unapproved discounts ("we can give you a special discount").
- A promise for a full group.
- A price from memory.
