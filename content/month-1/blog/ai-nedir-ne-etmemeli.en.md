# What an AI assistant should and should not do at a learning center

"Let the AI answer" is easy to say. But the day it states a wrong price or a lesson time that does not exist, trust is gone. You need clear limits.

## What AI does well

- **Understanding language.** Reading typo-filled messages like "qiymeti nedi", "ielts var?", "sabah ola biler?".
- **The first reply.** Greeting the customer and giving direction instantly, even at night or on a holiday.
- **Matching a course.** Asking age and goal to see which course fits.
- **Collecting details.** Name, phone number, course of interest.

## What AI should not decide alone

- **Price.** Read only from the approved table.
- **Open seats and dates.** Checked by program rules.
- **Discounts.** No promise without approval.
- **Duplicate registration.** Blocked by the program, by phone number.

## What should happen when it does not know

A good assistant can say "I do not know": "I will get the exact answer from our manager and write back." The manager sees that conversation as a notification.

## A checklist

1. Do answers come only from information you approved?
2. Is the seat checked before an enrollment is confirmed?
3. Does the assistant hand over to a person when it cannot answer?
4. Is each center's data separate from the others?
