# 5 steps to stop losing inquiries at your learning center

A parent writes on Instagram at 10:30 pm: "Do you have an IELTS course? How much is it?" The reply arrives at 11:00 the next morning. By then they have already read another center's answer.

Here are five simple steps that fit the daily work of a Baku learning center. We are not quoting made-up statistics: try each step for one week at your own center and see the result yourself.

## 1. Put all inquiries in one place

When WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram live on separate phones, nobody knows who is still waiting for an answer. Even one spreadsheet is enough: name, number, course of interest, channel, time.

## 2. Set a first-reply rule

Five minutes during working hours, an automatic reply at night. Even "We noted your question and will write by 10:00 am" beats silence.

## 3. Write price and schedule answers once

Keep ready texts for price, class days and group size. Typing from memory each time raises the risk of sending a wrong number.

## 4. Make the trial lesson the main next step

After the price talk, the next step should be "come and see a trial lesson". Offer a specific day and hour, not "whenever suits you".

## 5. Track who came and who did not

If someone enrolled but skipped the trial lesson, send one message the next day. It is the cheapest way to win them back.
